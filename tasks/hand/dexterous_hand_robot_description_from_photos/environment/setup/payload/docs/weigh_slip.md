# Weigh slip

Weighed by the lab before shipping: the modules on a bench scale before the hand was assembled, the hand after it
was assembled, a spare servo of the same model on a finer scale.

| item | scale resolution | mass |
|---|---|---|
| the whole hand, as shipped (without its box) | 1 g | 714 g |
| thumb module (the parts of group `thumb` in `parts/assembly.json`) | 1 g | 157 g |
| index finger module (group `index`) | 1 g | 126 g |
| middle finger module (group `middle`) | 1 g | 126 g |
| ring finger module (group `ring`) | 1 g | 126 g |
| one spare servo actuator VKR-4LBA, as delivered | 0.1 g | 30 g |
| the parts of that spare servo that turn with its output (its output horn and rear bearing), weighed apart | 0.1 g | 3.6 g |
