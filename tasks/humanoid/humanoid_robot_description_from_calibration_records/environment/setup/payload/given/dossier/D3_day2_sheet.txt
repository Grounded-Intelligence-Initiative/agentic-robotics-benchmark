Order of work: hip yaw (feet squared), hip roll (knee plates taped), ankles (level), then the feet squared again;
each Calibrate clicked at the end of its own step.

Steps 1 and 4, feet squared. A 1 m straightedge across the toes of both feet, set parallel to the flat of the
pelvis's front cover (part HM1_200_15C): a second straightedge held flat on the cover's face, and the two
perpendicular distances from it to the two ends of the 1 m straightedge made equal. The straight inner edge of each
sole plate (part HM1_600_39C on the left foot, HM1_500_39C on the right) squared to the 1 m straightedge with a try
square, to about 0.5 mm over the edge; one foot at a time, that hip-yaw drive in Damp, the rest holding; Calibrate.
Template hip-yaw rows set to 0. Done at step 1 and again at step 4.

Step 2, knee plates taped. Both hip-roll drives in Damp, the rest holding. Tape between the inner faces of the two
inner knee plates (part HM1_600_08A on the left knee, HM1_500_08A on the right), on each plate's centre line through
the knee axle, 5 mm inside its upper end and 5 mm inside its lower end: the upper gap and the lower gap set equal to
about 0.5 mm. Tape from the centre of the flat of the pelvis's front cover (part HM1_200_15C) to the knee axle's
centre on each plate: the two set equal to about 0.5 mm. Calibrate left, right. Template hip-roll rows set to 0. The
hips and knees were not touched between the taping and the levelling.

Step 3, ankles levelled. Each leg's levelling began with its hip-pitch and knee drives in Damp and the leg lifted
forward by hand, to bring the sole within reach of the level; the stack then held those two drives at the readings
they had at that moment (the lab's hold-here script, FN26). The legs so held were not at the zero posture, and their
posture was not written down. After both feet were levelled, and before step 4, both legs were sent back to the zero
posture. Left leg first, its ankle drives in Damp. Digital level (display to 0.1 deg; it repeats to about 0.1 deg)
on the flat of the pelvis's front cover (part HM1_200_15C), lying along its horizontal edge (roll) and standing on
its edge (pitch); then on the bottom of the bare sole plate (part HM1_600_39C on the left foot, HM1_500_39C on the
right), lengthwise (pitch) and crosswise (roll). The foot turned by hand until each sole reading equalled the
cover's; Calibrate Both; the ankle drives back on at the zero posture; hand off. Then the right leg. Differences,
sole minus cover: left pitch 0.0, left roll 0.0, right pitch 0.0, right roll 0.0 deg.
