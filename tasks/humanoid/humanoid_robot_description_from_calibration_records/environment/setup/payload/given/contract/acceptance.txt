ACCEPTANCE SHEET: the description files of unit 01

This sheet states how the two files you hand in are checked and scored. Every rule that can set the score or a part of
it to zero is printed here with its numbers.


1. WHAT YOU HAND IN

Two files in /home/user/submission/:
  unit.xml          the given 25-joint model file (robot_25dof.xml) corrected to the unit as crated
  calibration.yaml  the unit's home table and its two ankle maps (format unit-calibration/1, section 3)

No other file in the folder is read. Nothing submitted is executed: unit.xml is read as data and compiled by MuJoCo
3.14.0, and calibration.yaml is read by PyYAML's yaml.safe_load; no script, plugin or included file is run or opened.

check_submission.py beside this sheet runs the grader's own gate (gate.py beside it is the file the grader runs) on a
folder and prints every rule that fails. It knows nothing about the unit: a PASS says that the files can be scored,
not that they are right. A submission that fails the gate scores 0.


2. unit.xml

2.1 Fixed: the given model's tree. As compiled, your file keeps the given model's 26 link bodies (each by its name,
with the given parent, its position within 1e-6 m and its orientation within 1e-6 rad of the given ones in the
parent's frame; for pelvis_link that is its place in the world, where its free joint starts), the given model's 25
hinges (each by its name, on the given body, its axis within 1e-6 rad, the point its axis passes through within 1e-6 m
and its ref within 1e-6 rad of the given ones) and the free joint of pelvis_link; it has no other joint. So every
quantity below lives in the given model's link frames, and a joint angle means the same in your file as in the given
one.

2.2 Free. Everything else the whitelist allows: masses, centres of mass and inertias, geoms (primitives or the given
meshes), sites, joint ranges, and bodies without a joint added below a link (they move with that link). Only the
compiled model is read.

2.3 Read by the grader.
  - Every body's mass and centre of mass, as compiled.
  - Site "imu", on any body of the unit: its frame is the inertial unit's axes.
  - Sites "left_sole" and "right_sole", of type box, on left_ankle_roll_link and right_ankle_roll_link or on a body
    fixed below that link: the box's -z face is the foot's contact plane, and its x and y extents are the tread: the
    length and width of the flat bottom face of the foot as crated, the face that lies on a level plate, not the
    outline of the shoe.
  - The hinges and bodies through the fixed tree.

2.4 The whitelist. unit.xml is checked element by element before anything is compiled.
  Allowed and not read by the grader: visual (and its children), statistic, camera, light, sensor (and its children),
    material, texture (built in only), keyframe, option (any attribute), contact/exclude.
  Allowed and read: mujoco, compiler, default, asset/mesh (the given model's 26 mesh files only), worldbody, body,
    inertial, joint, freejoint, geom, site.
  Forbidden anywhere: actuator, attach, composite, custom, deformable, equality, extension, flex, flexcomp, frame,
    hfield, include, instance, numeric, pair, plugin, replicate, size, skin, tendon, text, tuple; and every element
    not listed below.
  Where each element may stand (any other place is refused):
    inside <mujoco>: <asset>, <compiler>, <contact>, <default>, <keyframe>, <option>, <sensor>, <statistic>, <visual>,
      <worldbody>
    inside <option>: <flag>
    inside <visual>: <global>, <headlight>, <map>, <quality>, <rgba>, <scale>
    inside <default>: <camera>, <default>, <geom>, <joint>, <light>, <material>, <mesh>, <site>
    inside <asset>: <material>, <mesh>, <texture>
    inside <material>: <layer>
    inside <worldbody>: <body>, <camera>, <geom>, <light>, <site>
    inside <body>: <body>, <camera>, <freejoint>, <geom>, <inertial>, <joint>, <light>, <site>
    inside <contact>: <exclude>
    inside <sensor>: any sensor element (none inside it)
    inside <keyframe>: <key>
  Compiler: angle="radian" must be stated; the attributes allowed: angle, assetdir, autolimits, balanceinertia,
    coordinate, eulerseq, exactmeshinertia, inertiafromgeom, inertiagrouprange, meshdir, strippath, texturedir,
    usethread; meshdir, assetdir and texturedir are allowed and ignored (the grader compiles the given meshes by file
    name); fusestatic, discardvisual and alignfree are refused.
  Refused attributes (unless false or 0): <compiler> fusestatic, discardvisual, alignfree; <freejoint> align; <body>
    mocap, gravcomp; <geom> fluidshape, fluidcoef.
  Meshes: a file attribute naming one of the given model's 26 mesh files (any folder: the file name decides); no
    inline data (vertex, normal, texcoord, face) and no built-in mesh (builtin, params).
  Textures: built in only (gradient, checker, flat); no file attribute.
  Every other attribute of an allowed element is free (no band on friction, contact or solver parameters: nothing is
    simulated).
  Caps: 64 bodies besides the world, 512 geoms, 64 sites; 1048576 bytes.


3. calibration.yaml (format unit-calibration/1)

One YAML document, a mapping of exactly these keys; any other key at any level is refused. Angles are in rad.

  schema: unit-calibration/1
  home_table: 25 rows in firmware slot order, row i = {slot: i, actuator, sign, home_rad}
    actuator   slot i's name, in slot order (below)
    sign       the integer 1 or -1
    home_rad   a finite number, |home_rad| <= 6.3
  ankle_map: {left: ..., right: ...}, each foot
    axes       [A, B]: row index i is motor A, column index j is motor B
    extent     [[-0.787, 0.787], [-0.787, 0.787]] (fixed)
    nodes      19 (fixed): node i lies at -0.787 + i x 1.574 / 18 rad on both axes
    pitch      19 rows of 19 numbers: the foot's pitch hinge angle (rad) at (A_i, B_j)
    roll       19 rows of 19 numbers: the foot's roll hinge angle (rad) at (A_i, B_j)
    every value finite, with |value| <= 1.6

The slots in order (0 to 24): L_hip_pitch, L_hip_roll, L_hip_yaw, L_knee, L_ankle_A, L_ankle_B, R_hip_pitch,
R_hip_roll, R_hip_yaw, R_knee, R_ankle_A, R_ankle_B, L_shoulder_pitch, L_shoulder_roll, L_shoulder_yaw, L_elbow,
L_wrist_yaw, R_shoulder_pitch, R_shoulder_roll, R_shoulder_yaw, R_elbow, R_wrist_yaw, waist_yaw, neck_yaw, neck_pitch.

The home table. A drive reports r, its output angle from its stored zero, in its own sense; the unit's stack reads
q = sign x r + home_rad (FN23 of the lab's notes). For the 21 joint drives q is the model file's joint angle. For an
ankle motor q is its coordinate in the firmware's ankle map, A = 2.02 pitch - 0.8 roll and B = -2.02 pitch - 0.8 roll
(pitch and roll: the model file's foot hinge angles).

The ankle maps. Between nodes a map is bilinear in A and B; outside the extent the bilinear formula of the nearest
edge cell is extended. The axes are the motors' q in the convention above; under it every command inside the
firmware's ankle box (pitch 0.35 rad, roll 0.1 rad) lies inside the extent. A table in another ankle convention moves
the readings and the firmware's clamp with it.

The file has at most 262144 bytes. It is read with yaml.safe_load after a scan of its tokens that refuses &-labels,
*-references and !-tags. YAML reads a number such as 1e-5 as text: write 1.0e-05.


4. THE GATE

The score is 0 when any of these rules fails (check_submission.py prints each failure under its rule's name):
  - files.folder: The submission folder exists and is a folder, not a symbolic link.
  - files.caps: The submission folder holds at most 20000 entries (files, folders, links and other entries, at any
      depth below it) and its regular files at most 209715200 bytes (200 MiB) in all, by the larger of their total
      size and the disk space they take: the grader's own measure, taken before it copies the folder and following no
      link. A folder beyond either cap is not copied and is graded as an empty folder, so that files.missing fails.
  - files.missing: unit.xml and calibration.yaml lie in the submission folder, each a regular file (not a symbolic
      link, a folder or a special file). No other file of the folder is read.
  - files.size: unit.xml has at most 1048576 bytes (1 MiB); calibration.yaml at most 262144 bytes (256 KiB).
  - files.late_write: Nothing in the submission folder was written after the session ended: the grader's own check of
      each entry's change and modification times against the moment the grading stage was set up (the checker cannot
      run it).
  - xml.declaration: unit.xml is UTF-8 text with no DOCTYPE and no ENTITY declaration.
  - xml.parse: unit.xml is well-formed XML and its root element is <mujoco>.
  - xml.forbidden: No element of the forbidden list appears anywhere: actuator, attach, composite, custom, deformable,
      equality, extension, flex, flexcomp, frame, hfield, include, instance, numeric, pair, plugin, replicate, size,
      skin, tendon, text, tuple (pair: the contact pair).
  - xml.unlisted: Every element is on the whitelist and sits where the whitelist allows it.
  - xml.compiler: Some <compiler> states angle="radian" and none states another unit; a compiler attribute is one of:
      angle, assetdir, autolimits, balanceinertia, coordinate, eulerseq, exactmeshinertia, inertiafromgeom,
      inertiagrouprange, meshdir, strippath, texturedir, usethread (meshdir, assetdir and texturedir are ignored).
  - xml.refused: These attributes are refused unless false or 0: fusestatic, discardvisual and alignfree on
      <compiler>, align on <freejoint>, mocap and gravcomp on <body>, fluidshape and fluidcoef on <geom>.
  - xml.mesh: Every <mesh> of the assets has a file attribute whose file name (its last path component) is one of the
      given model's 26 mesh files; none is given by inline data (vertex, normal, texcoord, face) or built in (builtin,
      params). The grader compiles its own copy of the named file.
  - xml.texture: Every <texture> is built in (builtin gradient, checker or flat) and names no file.
  - xml.caps: unit.xml has at most 64 bodies besides the world, 512 geoms and 64 sites.
  - model.compile: unit.xml compiles in MuJoCo 3.14.0 without an error (with the given meshes, its meshdir, assetdir
      and texturedir dropped).
  - model.warning: The compile raises no warning (MuJoCo warns, for example, when the XML contains a NaN).
  - tree.bodies: The given model's 26 link bodies are present, each by its name and with the given model's parent.
      Further bodies may be added; they carry no joint.
  - tree.frames: Each link body's position (within 1e-6 m) and orientation (within 1e-6 rad) in its parent's frame
      equal the given model's.
  - tree.joints: There are exactly 26 joints: the given model's 25 hinges, each by its name, a hinge and on its given
      body, and one free joint, on pelvis_link.
  - tree.hinges: Each hinge's axis (within 1e-6 rad), its position, the point its axis passes through (within 1e-6 m),
      and its ref (within 1e-6 rad) equal the given model's.
  - sites.imu: A site named imu lies on a body of the unit: pelvis_link, a link below it, or a body fixed to one.
  - sites.sole: Sites left_sole and right_sole are of type box, on left_ankle_roll_link and right_ankle_roll_link or
      on a body fixed below that link (no joint in between).
  - mass.total: The total mass is at least 20 kg and at most 60 kg.
  - mass.link: Each of the 26 link bodies has a mass above 0 kg. A body added below a link may have no mass of its
      own.
  - numbers.finite: Every compiled number the grader reads is finite: each body's mass, centre of mass, inertia frame
      and principal inertias, and the position, orientation and size of the sites imu, left_sole and right_sole.
  - yaml.encoding: calibration.yaml is UTF-8 text.
  - yaml.properties: calibration.yaml uses no &-label, no *-reference and no !-tag (YAML's node labels, references to
      them and type tags).
  - yaml.parse: calibration.yaml is one YAML document that yaml.safe_load reads.
  - cal.top: The document is a mapping of exactly three keys: schema, home_table, ankle_map.
  - cal.schema: schema is the text unit-calibration/1.
  - cal.home_table: home_table is a list of exactly 25 rows, row i for firmware slot i.
  - cal.row: Row i is a mapping of exactly slot, actuator, sign, home_rad: slot is the integer i, actuator slot i's
      name, sign the integer 1 or -1, home_rad a finite number with |home_rad| <= 6.3.
  - cal.ankle_map: ankle_map is a mapping of exactly left and right; each foot a mapping of exactly axes, extent,
      nodes, pitch and roll.
  - cal.foot: Per foot: axes is [A, B]; extent is [[-0.787, 0.787], [-0.787, 0.787]]; nodes is 19; pitch and roll are
      each 19 rows of 19 finite numbers with |value| <= 1.6 (rad).
  - gate.internal: The gate ran to its end (an unexpected error inside it is reported under this code).


5. THE SCORE

  score = gate x M x D
  L(e) = clip(ln(zero / e) / ln(zero / full), 0, 1)        e: one error; full and zero: its band below

A term is the mean of L over its samples (an error that is not finite scores 0); a family is the mean of its terms; M
is the weighted sum of the families; D is the mean over the postures of flat x weight x upright. A description that
equals the unit scores 1.

5.1 M: 240 samples of the unit's bus over its range of motion. Each sample is the unit at one posture with its pelvis
at one attitude: the 25 readings and the gravity vector of the inertial unit that it gives. Your angles are your home
table and ankle maps applied to the readings; your kinematics are MuJoCo's on your compiled unit.xml.

  Each family with its weight; each term with its unit, its band and its error:
  feet (weight 0.4)
    position (mm; full 5.9, zero 34.1): per sample and foot, the distance in the pelvis link's frame between your
      files' and the unit's position of the tread centre (a fixed point of the ankle-roll link)
    normal (deg; full 1.03, zero 4.07): per sample and foot, the angle between your files' and the unit's ankle-roll z
      axes in the pelvis link's frame
    heading (deg; full 0.65, zero 2.57): per sample and foot, the rotation about the unit's sole normal between your
      files' and the unit's ankle-roll x axes
  arms (weight 0.1)
    elbow (deg; full 0.195, zero 2.72): per sample and side, the forearm's attitude against the upper arm (the elbow),
      your files against the unit
  trunk (weight 0.2)
    attitude (deg; full 0.115, zero 3): per sample, the angle between the gravity in the torso link's
      (waist_yaw_link's) frame that your imu site and kinematics predict from the sample's gravity vector, and the
      unit's
  weight (weight 0.2)
    centre of mass (mm; full 3.9, zero 34.9): at each of the 10 postures' reading vectors as planned on the unit, your
      files' centre of mass in your feet frame against the unit's in its feet frame (horizontal)
  sole (weight 0.1)
    plane (mm; full 0.28, zero 5): per foot, the distance along the unit's sole normal, at its tread centre, from its
      tread plane to your contact plane
    footprint (mm; full 0.49, zero 19): per foot, the largest distance from a corner of either tread rectangle to the
      nearest corner of the other

  In the position term the tread centre is the unit's own point, fixed in the ankle-roll link's frame (the centre of
  the unit's tread): your files place it by your angles and kinematics, the unit by its own, and your sole sites do
  not enter that term.

  The feet frame (here and in D): the origin at the midpoint of the two tread centres; x the mean of the two
  ankle-roll links' x axes projected on the plate (the horizontal), normalised; z up.

5.2 D: 10 static postures, planned by the grader on your files and set down by the unit.
  - Planning, on your files: each sole box's -z face flat on a level plate, the feet at set positions with yaw 0, the
    pelvis at the posture's drop, pitch and shift. Each leg by inverse kinematics on your tree (least-squares Newton
    on six degrees of freedom, at most 60 iterations from a fixed start). The readings through your home table; the
    ankle motors through the inverse of your ankle maps (Newton from the inverse of the firmware's linear map, a
    finite-difference Jacobian with step 1e-6, at most 40 iterations to 1e-12); each ankle command clamped to pitch
    0.35 rad and roll 0.1 rad in your file's firmware coordinates, as the firmware clamps it.
  - The unit at those readings: its own home table; each ankle's motors through their own senses and zeros and the
    ankle's two-rod linkage as crated; a foot attitude beyond the ankle's mechanical range moved to the nearest
    attitude inside it; the 21 joints stopped at their mechanical stops; one rigid body.
  - Set down: the body held at the plan's pelvis attitude and lowered onto the level plate; then it comes to rest by
    quasi-static toppling (about the first contact point, then the contact edge, in the sense gravity tips it, until
    the centre of mass lies over the hull of the touching corners; a corner touches within 1e-7 m; each rotation exact
    to the next contact; at most 200 steps).

  Per posture, flat x weight x upright, each an L of its error:
    flat (deg; full 0.94, zero 4.29): the worse sole's tilt to the plate at the first contact
    weight (mm; full 6.5, zero 24.3): the unit's centre of mass at rest, in its feet frame at rest, against the one
      your files predict in the planned feet frame (horizontal)
    upright (deg; full 0.525, zero 4): the torso link's z axis at rest against the planned one

5.3 Rules that set a posture or a sample to 0.
  - A posture whose leg inverse kinematics leaves a residual above 1e-9 (m or rad) scores 0.
  - A posture whose ankle-map inverse fails (|det J| below 1e-12, a value that is not finite, or a residual above
    1e-9) scores 0.
  - A fall scores 0: more than 25 deg of rotation while coming to rest, balanced on a point, or no further contact.
  - An error that is not finite scores 0 for its sample.
