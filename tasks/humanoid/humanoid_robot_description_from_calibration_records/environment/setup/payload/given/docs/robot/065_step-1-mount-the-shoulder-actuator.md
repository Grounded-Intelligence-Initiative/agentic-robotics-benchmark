# Step 1: Mount the Shoulder Actuator

> Mount actuator ID 13 in part 400_04B and secure it with threadlocked M4x8 Flat bolts.

- Section: Humanoid › Assemble the humanoid robot › Assemble › Left Arm Module › Shoulder Joint Assembly

## Parts needed

| Part | Count |
| - | -: |
| RJ-6381 actuator (part `400_17X`) (ID 13) | 1 |
| `400_04B` | 1 |
| M4x8 Flat bolt | 12 |
| Threadlocker | As needed |

## Instructions

1. Put the RJ-6381 actuator (ID 13) on a flat surface, the port should be at the bottom, and the actuator shaft should be upward toward the ceiling.

2. Take Part `400_04B` and put the actuator through the center of the part, and align the holes on the actuator with the holes around the circumference of the Part `400_04B`.

3. Using **12 of** M4x8 Flat bolts, tighten each of them lightly into the holes, to keep the actuator and Part `400_04B` aligned first.

4. After the parts are aligned:

   > [!NOTE]
   >
   > **Not ready to apply threadlocker?**
   >
   > You can assemble without threadlocker while you check the fit, alignment, and orientation. When you are confident, disassemble as needed, apply threadlocker to the bolts, and reassemble them following these instructions.

   - remove **12 of** M4x8 Flat bolts one at a time in a star formation,
   - apply threadlocker,
   - then tighten them back to secure Part `400_04B` and the actuator together.
