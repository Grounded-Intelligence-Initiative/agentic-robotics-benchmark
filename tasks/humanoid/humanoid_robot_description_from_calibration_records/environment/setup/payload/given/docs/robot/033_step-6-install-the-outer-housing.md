# Step 6: Install the Outer Housing

> Enclose the assembly with parts 600_07C and 600_04C and secure it with 17 bolts.

- Section: Humanoid › Assemble the humanoid robot › Assemble › Left Leg Module › Thigh Assembly

## Parts needed

| Part | Count |
| - | -: |
| Current assembly from Step 5 | 1 |
| `600_07C` | 1 |
| `600_04C` | 1 |
| M4x14 Socket bolt | 17 |

## Instructions

1. Take the current assembly and lay it flat on the surface such that part `600_12A` is facing upward toward the ceiling and toward you.

2. Take part `600_07C` (it should have 8 holes, not to confuse with part `600_04C`) and prepare to align to `600_03C` as shown below.

3. Take **8 of** M4x14 Socket bolts and lightly tighten them into the holes on parts `600_07C` to align the part to the threaded insert on part `600_03C`.

4. Take part `600_04C` (it should have 9 holes) and prepare to align to `600_03C` as shown below.

5. Take **9 of** M4x14 Socket bolts and lightly tighten them into the holes on parts `600_04C` to align the part to the threaded insert on part `600_03C`.

6. Once both parts are aligned, fully tighten all **17 of** M4x14 Socket bolts in a star formation to secure part `600_07C` and part `600_04C` to part `600_03C`.
