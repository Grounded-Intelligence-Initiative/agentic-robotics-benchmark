# Step 7: Attach the Actuator Hub

> Route the actuator cable through part 500_01B and fasten the part to actuator ID 9.

- Section: Humanoid › Assemble the humanoid robot › Assemble › Right Leg Module › Thigh Assembly

## Parts needed

| Part | Count |
| - | -: |
| Current assembly from Step 6 | 1 |
| `500_01B` | 1 |
| M4x25 Socket bolt | 5 |
| Threadlocker | As needed |
| Super glue | As needed |

## Instructions

1. Take the current assembly and turn it such that the RJ-5378 actuator (part `500_02X`) (ID 9) is facing toward you.

2. Take part `500_01B` and locate the inner part that is not hollow. Put **5 of** M4x25 Socket bolts into the 5 holes on part `500_01B`.

3. Route the cable from the hollow shaft through the center hole in part `500_01B`, then through the L-shaped cable channel until it exits the other side. Check the pictures below to show how the routing is done on real pieces.

   

   

   

4. Align all the M4x25 Socket bolts with the threaded holes on the actuator and then lightly tighten the bolts into the actuator to align part `500_01B` with the actuator.

5. After the parts are aligned:

   > [!NOTE]
   >
   > **Not ready to apply threadlocker?**
   >
   > You can assemble without threadlocker while you check the fit, alignment, and orientation. When you are confident, disassemble as needed, apply threadlocker to the bolts, and reassemble them following these instructions.

   - remove **5 of** M4x25 Socket bolts one at a time in a star formation,
   - apply threadlocker,
   - then tighten them back to secure Part `500_01B` and the actuator together.

6. You can choose between this two option:

   1. Take the given tape and apply it across the wire.

      

   2. Take the super glue and apply one drip at the place shown in the images below to keep the cable flush. This is required before installing the leg to the pelvis.
