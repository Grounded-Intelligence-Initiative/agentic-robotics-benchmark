LAB NOTES: THE LAB'S CONTROL STACK (unit 01)

The notes of the stack that drove unit 01 on day 2: the firmware notes that apply on the lab's stack, the lab's own
notes FN23 to FN26 and the conventions of the records. Angles are in radians unless a note says otherwise; the model
file is the maker's 25-joint model, robot_25dof.xml.

FIRMWARE NOTES ON THE LAB'S STACK

FN1   latch margin 0.02 rad beyond the limit of FN24 in firmware coordinates (ankles: pitch / roll of the reported
      motors through the inverse linear map), any mode, every cycle, one sample; the latch acts on the REPORTED
      reading (the reply's position, truncated to its step: FN19), not on the true angle
FN3   STAND pose in the 25-joint model's angles: hip pitch L -0.15 / R +0.15, knee +0.45 / -0.45, ankle pitch
      (firmware) -0.30 / +0.30, shoulder pitch -0.25 / +0.25, roll -0.05 / +0.05, elbow +0.40 / -0.40, wrist yaw
      +0.05 / -0.05 (each 0.05 rad inside its limit in the model file), the rest 0; cosine ramp 2 s
FN4   gains (kp N m/rad / kd N m s/rad): hips, knees 150 / 5; waist 65 / 5; shoulder pitch 57 / 5, roll 86 / 5, yaw
      96 / 5; elbow, wrist 40 / 2; neck 40 / 2; ankle motors 27.5 / 1.25 each
FN5   ankle gains per drive in motor units; the maker's policy card prints the joint-space equivalents
      224.42 / 10.201 (pitch) and 35.20 / 1.600 (roll)
FN6   damping constants: in DAMP, in FAULT_DAMP and with kp 0: KP 0 and KD = FN4's kd of the slot (a kd sent with kp
      0 is replaced); kd 0 with kp above 0: KP as sent, KD = FN4's kd of the slot
FN7   inertial axes: the axes of the inertial chip as mounted on the motion control board; the board's axes are
      parallel to the body it is screwed to: x forward, y left, z up with the unit at the model's zero, within
      0.1 deg
FN8   attitude filter: revised Madgwick at 1 kHz on the inertial unit's gyro and accelerometer, gain 0.5,
      acceleration rejection 10 deg, initialised from the accelerometer at boot
FN9   timing: cycle 2 ms; a command is taken at the first cycle at or after its arrival plus the command delay;
      state every 5 ms, its timestamp_us counted from the firmware's first boot (a restart does not reset it);
      alerts every 20th packet
FN10  clamps: positions to the stack's limits (FN24) (ankles before the map); kp 0-500, kd 0-5 with INVALID_GAINS; a
      non-finite gain array replaced by the defaults
FN11  trajectories in DAMP or an unarmed STAND: dropped, unreported
FN12  2 s trajectory timeout: DAMP, not latched, no alert; STAND accepted again
FN13  reported temperature = winding node; warning 60 C, latch 80 C
FN14  KT register = the maker's Kt; segmented compensation off
FN15  velocity target and feed-forward torque 0
FN17  command fields (humanoid.io.RobotCommand), field by field: mode (1): DAMP (0) is never dropped, whatever its
      other fields and its protocol_version (the maker: "never dropped - always accepted"); STAND (1) is a stand
      request, also with a payload, and is dropped while fault-damped (FAULT_DAMP); MOVE (2) needs a trajectory
      payload: a MOVE without a payload is dropped; FAULT_DAMP (5) or any other value: dropped. command_control (2):
      POLICY (0) or TRAJECTORY (1): a trajectory whose command_control does not match its payload is dropped (the
      field's default, 0, is POLICY: a trajectory must carry TRAJECTORY). policy (3): dropped (MOVE/POLICY is not
      installed on the lab's units). all_trajectory (5): positions, kp, kd, 25 entries each (FN6, FN10, FN11);
      positions not 25 or not all finite: dropped. eol (9): dropped. timestamp_us (6): ignored. sequence (7): echoed
      in RobotState.last_cmd_sequence by every command the firmware takes (one taken and ignored in FAULT_DAMP
      included), never by a dropped one. protocol_version (8): a command with any value but 1 is dropped, a DAMP
      excepted. firmware_epoch_id (11): ignored (FN18). Bytes that do not parse are dropped. Every drop is silent
FN18  firmware_epoch_id: ignored on intake; RobotState carries the boot count
FN19  command and reply fields encoded by truncation: code = floor((x - lo) (2^b - 1) / (hi - lo)) of the value
      clipped to [lo, hi], read back as lo + code (hi - lo) / (2^b - 1), so LSB = (hi - lo) / (2^b - 1); command KP
      uint12 over 0-500 (LSB 0.1221), KD uint9 over 0-5 (LSB 0.009785), position uint16 over +-12.5 rad (LSB
      3.815e-4 rad), speed uint12 over +-18 rad/s (LSB 8.791e-3 rad/s); replies: position and speed the same, phase
      current uint12 over +- the model's peak phase current (LSB 0.01465 A for the RJ-5660 and RJ-5669, 0.02930 A
      RJ-8867, 0.004884 A RJ-5378, 0.009768 A RJ-6381), temperature uint8 = 2 T + 50 (0.5 C steps); so the zero
      speed target is held as -0.004396 rad/s, FN4's kp 150 as 149.939, and a reply reads on average half a step
      below the value
FN20  drive heartbeat: the firmware writes every drive's frame every cycle; a drive that receives no frame for 0.5 s
      disables itself (no current: friction and stops only), and the next frame after such a time-out re-enables it
FN21  alert fields of the state stream (humanoid.io.Alert; at most 48 active, one entry per alert id): severity =
      id // 16 (0 critical, 1 warning, 2 info); value and threshold = the nearest integer (ties to even) to 1000 |x|
      of the alert's quantity x in its unit, clipped to the uint32 range, NaN as 0: JOINT_OUT_OF_RANGE the reading
      in rad (the ankles: the firmware's pitch or roll, FN1) and the latch threshold it passed (the limit of
      FN24 +- 0.02 rad), MOTOR_TEMP_HIGH and MOTOR_OVERTEMP the reported temperature and 60 / 80 C, FALL_DETECTED
      the tilt acos(-g_z) of the delayed gravity reading and the tilt at g_z = -0.5 (60 deg), INVALID_GAINS the
      first clamped gain as sent and its maximum (500 N m/rad or 5 N m s/rad), MOTOR_COMM_LOSS 1 and 1 (cycles), an
      injected fault its injected value and threshold; source_id = the drive slot (0 for a fall); first_set_us = 500
      x the ticks from the firmware's first boot to the tick it was first raised (the clock of timestamp_us, FN9);
      error_flags: bit 0 latched, bit 1 + id for each critical alert raised since boot

THE LAB'S OWN NOTES

FN23  readout convention: Calibrate stores the drive's present output angle as its zero, in the drive. A drive
      reports r, its output angle from that zero, in its own sense. The lab's stack holds one home table per unit:
      q = sign x r + home. For the 21 joint drives q is the model file's joint angle, and sign is +1 when r grows as
      the joint turns in the positive sense about the model file's joint axis. For an ankle motor q is its
      coordinate in the firmware's map (A = 2.02 pitch - 0.8 roll, B = -2.02 pitch - 0.8 roll; pitch and roll are
      the model file's foot hinge angles): sign is +1 when r grows as the motor turns the way that raises its own
      coordinate, the other motor still, and home is that coordinate of the foot's attitude at the moment the
      motor's zero was stored. The stream reports q, the ankles as their two motors; commands are 25 targets in q
      (ankle slots: pitch and roll, clamped at 0.35 and 0.10 rad, then mapped). Limits and latches act on q. Every
      record says whether it is in r or in q under the template.
FN24  the stack's limits on q: on the 21 joint drives the model file's ranges widened by 0.1 rad at each end, the
      two wrist yaws' by 0.35 rad at each end; on the ankles the firmware box, 0.35 rad pitch and 0.10 rad roll;
      latch margin FN1.
FN25  template home table: The stack cannot run without a home table. For a partner's unit the lab fills a template
      from the model file: where the maker's procedure stores a zero at an end stop, the home is the model file's
      limit on that side; elsewhere 0; ankle motors 0; signs from the jog check.

      it sends that drive's present reading (q, as the state stream reports it: FN23) as the drive's target, at the
      stack's gains for that drive (FN4), in the stack's trajectory command (FN17); every other drive keeps the
      target and gains it had.

THE RECORDS

- Every recorded gravity vector is in the inertial unit's axes (FN7).
- A recorded gravity vector is the attitude filter's estimate of the direction of gravity (FN8): a unit vector,
  (0, 0, -1) when the inertial unit's z axis points straight up.
- The inertial unit is the inertial chip on the motion control board. In a model of the unit its site is where that
  chip sits in the unit as built: the board's place in the unit and the chip's place on the board decide it; its
  axes are those of FN7.
- The gantry chain splits into two legs to the two shoulder shackles.
