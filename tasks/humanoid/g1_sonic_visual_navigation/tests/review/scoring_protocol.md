# Visual navigation measurement protocol

Sixteen independent physical resets are drawn from verifier-owned operating-system entropy. For each turn sign, eight first distances, turn magnitudes and final distances are independently shuffled stratified draws in the ranges in grader_config.json. World reset and probe order use separate draws. Public seed 7 and all controller development results are separate from official measurement.

Each route requires an upright first stop and hold, a signed in-place pivot and a final stop under the physical thresholds in instruction.md. Hitting the final stop is a single success. The denominator includes every scheduled course, including aborts, falls and timeouts. Report the raw success fraction, both directions, individual stage completions, seconds of simulated motion, FSQ cost and measured joint acceleration.

At the first visible pivot decision the alternate scene flips the sign of the signal and moves its yellow landmark. Once near a braking point on each walking leg the alternate scene shifts the waypoint center along its real axis within the sampled range. All alternatives use the real current camera pose and unmodified joint, velocity and decoder history. At every public five-tick boundary, from reset through termination and regardless of progress, the verifier submits live, alternate, same-scene relit and one repeated packet in a randomized order. Other decisions have one live query. The physical thresholds select which available visual responses to score and affect only verifier-side logging. A qualifying change of light is synchronized to the next five-tick boundary (up to four additional settled rows). Each live frame is acquired once per decision tick. A separate renderer serves counterfactual views. The nuisance frame re-renders RGB with altered lamp brightness at the unchanged pose and reuses the same live depth acquisition, keeping the intervention confined to appearance. Only the live action drives the physical episode. A camera intervention that is entirely out of frame supplies no response evidence. A first-row change requires at least two differing integer coordinates.

For each kind, subtract the larger of the identical-repeat and relighting change fractions from the semantic change fraction. Require at least 8 observed turn checks and 16 waypoint checks, a signed-cue net of at least 0.50 and a range net of at least 0.25. Exact-repeat change fractions must each be at most 0.15. Failing a gate yields zero score. Physical results remain visible for diagnosis.

The first official null-anchor calibration uses the submitted frozen reference and untouched controller. Anchor value is the measured strict fraction on all 16 assigned reference starts. The number needed for full credit is max(14, min(15, calibration successes − 1)). Calibration requires both perception checks, at least 14 physical successes and at least one strict success in each direction. With an accepted numeric anchor and both feedback checks satisfied, reward is the successful course count divided by the full-credit count, capped at one. The same frozen reference and physical grader must then pass an independent ordinary ALE validation. The procedure preserves raw physical outcomes on every run.
