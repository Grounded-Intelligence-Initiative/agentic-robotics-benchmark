# Obstacle course calibration protocol

The verifier draws sixteen courses from fresh entropy after the submitted controller is installed. Four courses occupy each combination of positive or negative requested pivot and first opening on the robot's left or right. There are four single-gate, six double-gate same-side and six double-gate opposite-side courses. Each of the four cells has one example of each family and a fourth double-gate example. A fair random diagonal assigns those two extra same-side and two extra opposite-side courses, balancing each pivot and each first-gap side. Every layout is newly generated. Only the required gate count and sides are conditioned. Gate positions, opening offsets and other geometry retain the conditional distribution of the public generator. Course distance, final leg, turn magnitude, robot initialization and obstacle initialization come from separate draws. The four draws per cell independently stratify each continuous course parameter within the limits recorded in `grader_config.json`. The verifier shuffles the sixteen cases.

## Physical score

Each controller starts as a newly launched process and receives only current native camera and robot-local measurements. The simulator sends one live observation per decision, accepts up to five applied rows and steps the real body through ten 2 ms contact samples per applied row. A full course earns one success; partial crossings, a stop alone and action changes contribute zero. The numerator requires all of the following in the same continuous rollout.

* Every required gate records its first forward crossing of the barrier midplane, in gate order. The interpolated pelvis center must be strictly inside the correct opening. The reported clearance is its distance to the nearer lateral opening edge. It measures pelvis crossing position. Full-body obstacle clearance is checked independently by the collision engine.
* Every integrated substep remains free of robot–obstacle contact, including after the center crosses the barrier. The verifier records the count of 2 ms contact-bearing samples, the first such sample time and the action tick. The approach must also remain within the physical course boundary.
* The approach, signed pivot and final departure must each reach the native upright, supported, quiet stop and dwell. Their accepted stage records must be in order and the simulation must register final native success. Falls, collisions, missed gates, lost holds, time limits and controller aborts remain in the denominator.

The reported raw score is strict course completions divided by sixteen. The metric for actual simulated seconds integrates time until each terminal. Native action rows, 64 FSQ coordinates per applied row, within-episode coordinate changes and pooled 29-joint acceleration over 0.10 s differences are recorded separately. Per-trial numeric fields identify each requested turn and distance, gate signs and timestamps, contact counts, physical stops and actual motion costs. Independent optical interventions, when reported separately, measure visual dependence across matched physical runs. The official reward uses the actual course only.

## Prospective numerical rule

Freeze the scene, submitted reference and roster generator before opening two independently initialized official reference measurements with the anchor unset. Both measurements must complete without solver aborts and achieve at least fifteen of sixteen strict courses. Both pivot directions and all three gate families must have a strict completion in each measurement. If a measurement misses this criterion, retain it and revise the implementation before any subsequent attempt receives a new registration.

The first accepted bootstrap raw fraction sets the anchor. The lower strict completion count of these two independent calibration panels sets the saturation count. When both achieve sixteen, full credit requires sixteen. If either achieves fifteen, full credit requires fifteen. The saturation ratio in the anchor record is the saturation count divided by the bootstrap completion count. An accepted release then needs two fresh ordinary ALE validations with numeric anchor frozen, untouched reward zero and oracle reward one. All raw counts, failures and source identities remain reportable.
