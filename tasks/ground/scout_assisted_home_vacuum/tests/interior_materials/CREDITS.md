# Indoor materials

Six downsampled diffuse textures from Poly Haven, licensed under
[CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license):
[Herringbone Parquet](https://polyhaven.com/a/herringbone_parquet),
[Laminate Floor 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/laminate_floor_03),
[Terrazzo Tiles](https://polyhaven.com/a/terrazzo_tiles), and
[Painted Plaster Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_plaster_wall),
[Denim Fabric](https://polyhaven.com/a/denim_fabric) and
[Oak Veneer 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_02).
The source checksums and resizing procedure are recorded in
`../diagnostics/import_finishes.py`. Each room applies its own finish tint.
Wall prints, frames, floating shelves, windows, sconces and skirting are
original procedural scene elements. No stock photograph is used as a room render.
