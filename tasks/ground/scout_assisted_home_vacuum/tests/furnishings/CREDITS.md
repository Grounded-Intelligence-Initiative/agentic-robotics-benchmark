# Furniture mesh sources

These 18 meshes are adapted from [Kenney's Furniture Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit),
distributed by the author on [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/furniture-kit)
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Original archive: [Furniture Pack 1.0](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/kenney_furniturePack.zip).
The importer in `../diagnostics/import_furniture.py` pins the archive digest, rotates the
coordinate system, normalizes bounds and maps the original material groups into a compact
palette. No downloads are required during an episode. Architecture and surface patterns
are generated by the benchmark; the meshes are not photographs or scanned assets.
