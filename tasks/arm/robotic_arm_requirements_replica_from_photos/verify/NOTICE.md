# NOTICE (grader-side; never staged into the agent's home)

The six-servo desktop arm of this task is a hidden VARIANT of the SO-ARM101 arm by TheRobotStudio / Hugging Face LeRobot
(https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100, commit 5f6d2b876a53a4872e405b991dd925556c9e38a4, Apache-2.0), generated by
the information tier's GT-world generator (`docs/plan/arm/chain/shared/arm/gt_arm/`, lane W0-GT): the printed parts are
modified derivatives of the repository's STEP files (B-rep band stretches of the upper arm, forearm, base, shoulder and
fingers, re-levelled link frames, recoloured), the servo is a parametric stand-in of the Feetech STS3215 class named
`BUS-SERVO-12V-A` in the tier. Modification statement (Apache-2.0 section 4(b)): every printed part of the hidden and
the practice arms differs from the source parts by the variant perturbations recorded in the generator's
`perturbation.json`; the MuJoCo model follows the layout of MuJoCo Menagerie `robotstudio_so101` (commit c96a32d,
Apache-2.0). CITATION: Knight, Kooijmans, Cadene et al., "Standard Open SO-100 & SO-101 Arms", 2024.

Rung I2 (lane W1-REQ-I2): the wrist-roll and / or jaw pockets of an instance may hold a COMPACT body, a parametric
scaling of the same stand-in servo (0.80 in plan about its horn axis, 0.88 in height about its horn face) held in a printed
cradle (`shared/arm/req_info/i2/compact.py`: a post-build patch of the generator's GT arm, recorded as an interim path in
the lane report because the generator has no servo body classes). The kitchen scale, its seven-segment display and the
second steel rule are generated from numbers by `shared/arm/req_info/i2/bench_scene.py`.

The objects on the bench are the CC0 procedural kitchen library of `shared/arm/kitchen/assets.py`; the scale references
and textures are generated from numbers by `shared/render/`. The vendors (Kestrel Motion, Altair Drives, PowerDesk,
Bus Tools), their part ids and every datasheet number are fictional (modelled on public hobby bus-servo classes); rung I2
re-drew every number of the rung-I1 catalogue and added the compact ladder. `gt/catalogue.json` is the generator-side
truth of the pages (`shared/arm/req_info/i2/catalogue.py`); no agent-visible code carries these numbers.

The task's structure (hidden ground-truth world observed through one fixed photo set with physical scale references,
practice instances with their own ground truth) follows the owner's information-acquisition direction of 2026-09-27
(`docs/plan/arm/chain/info_tier/PLAN.md`). Per the owner's rule the givens, the instruction and the public card never name
the arm, its repositories, Menagerie, EmbodiedSWE or the servo vendor; this file and the -dev folder carry the attribution.
