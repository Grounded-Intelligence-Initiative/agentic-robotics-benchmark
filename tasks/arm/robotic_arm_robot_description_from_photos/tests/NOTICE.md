# NOTICE (grader-side; never staged into the agent's home)

Every arm of this task (the photographed build whose ground truth sits in `tests/gt/`, the oracle description in
`solution/answer/`, and the published parts in the agent's `parts/`) is a modified derivative of the open-hardware
SO-ARM101 follower arm by TheRobotStudio / Hugging Face LeRobot (https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100, commit
5f6d2b876a53a4872e405b991dd925556c9e38a4, Apache-2.0), generated by the arm ground-truth generator
(gt-arm/1.4.0, profile `rd3`). Modification
statement (Apache-2.0 section 4(b)): the ten printed parts were re-exported as B-rep solids in world-aligned link frames
at a straight-up zero pose; the nominal member (the task's "published parts") keeps the public geometry under neutral
names; the photographed build was perturbed by the generator, which may stretch the base, shoulder, upper arm, forearm,
wrist and fingers within bands, change the shoulder offset and add printed spacer blocks in servo pockets and cut-outs,
then applies the unit tolerances of the build (beam scales, wrist-to-tip, joint-frame errors, pitch-axis tilts, jaw pad),
re-levelled so that the zero pose stays straight up; recoloured; the servo calibration layer (bus zeros, drive
directions, firmware limits written independently of the zeros) is drawn by the generator. The MuJoCo description
follows the layout of MuJoCo Menagerie `robotstudio_so101` (commit c96a32d, Apache-2.0); collision hulls (CoACD) and
inertials are the generator's. The derived material is distributed under the same licence terms. CITATION: Knight,
Kooijmans, Cadene et al., "Standard Open SO-100 & SO-101 Arms", 2024.

The servo is the neutral `BUS-SERVO-12V-A`, a parametric stand-in generated from datasheet numbers; no vendor geometry
is shipped. The photos are rendered with NVIDIA OVRTX (RTX path tracing) on the authoring host, the arm in a photo cove
generated from numbers (one seamless matte grey surface, three softboxes); the segmentation used for the photo check
comes from MuJoCo (Apache-2.0). The steel rule, the can, the card, the printed cover plates over the pitch-axis brackets
and their textures are generated from numbers by the photo pipeline.

Per the owner's rule the givens, the instruction and the public card never name the arm, its repositories, Menagerie or
the servo vendor; this file carries the attribution.
