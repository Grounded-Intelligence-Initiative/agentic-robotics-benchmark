# Scale references (nominal sizes)

Three everyday objects of known size were put on the bench for some of the photos; `photos/photos.json` lists, per photo,
which of them are on the bench (`scale_refs`). Where they lay was not recorded. Nothing else on the bench or in the room
has a known size; the servos' dimensions are in `servo_datasheet.md` / `servo_drawing.png`.

- Steel rule: 300 mm scale (1 mm ticks along one long edge, longer ticks every 5 mm and 10 mm, numbered every 10 mm from
  0 to 30 cm), overall 316 x 25 x 0.9 mm, lying flat on the bench.
- Beverage can (standard 355 ml size): 66 mm diameter, 122 mm tall, standing on the bench.
- ID-1 plastic card (bank-card size, ISO/IEC 7810): 85.60 x 53.98 x 0.76 mm, corner radius 3.18 mm, lying flat on the
  bench.
