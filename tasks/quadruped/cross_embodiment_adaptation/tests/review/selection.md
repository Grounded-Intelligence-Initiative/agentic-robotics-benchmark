# V6 fixed suite

V6 keeps all eight official seeds byte-for-byte from the v5 parent
`6cf591c54cf56c7734923c76d4d9b7d85a2f6a4d`. Seed bits 14 and 15 independently
set the wide passage side at the first and last rack. This bit pair was chosen
for 2/2/2/2 configuration coverage before the final controls, based on seed bits
alone. The rest of the geometry and the Go1 variation are drawn from the
seeded generator. There is no outcome filter, reroll, or per-site exception.

| Site | Wide passage sides, west to east |
|---|---|
| 0 | South, North |
| 1 | South, South |
| 2 | North, South |
| 3 | North, North |
| 4 | North, South |
| 5 | South, South |
| 6 | North, North |
| 7 | South, North |

The middle opening forces a return toward center for all four combinations.
At each choice, either side of the short route can be walled off; the short
route itself is never visually closed. Its aperture is narrower than the
nominal opposite-thigh-axis spacing. Both alternatives are physical gaps
between identically painted collision boxes, not semantic labels.

The previous generation and measurements are historical and remain available
at the parent revision; the v4 notes and archived suites are not v6 evidence.
