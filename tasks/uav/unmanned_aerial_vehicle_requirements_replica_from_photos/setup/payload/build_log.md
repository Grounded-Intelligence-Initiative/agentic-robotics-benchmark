# Lab build log / unit U-17
BL-01. Weighed dry core: 785 g. This includes fabricated plates, printed parts, mounts/cage, wires, flight controller,
compute and their electronics. It excludes four motors, four propellers, ESC, battery, scanner and ballast pod.
For customer sizing, distribute this mass uniformly over the fabricated core bounding box, centred as B-09 says.
The three core dimensions exclude added scanner, battery cage and ballast fixtures.

BL-02. Carbon-fibre plates and TPU adapters follow the AgiPix construction; this build is a scaled fabrication.
The initial traveller copied the upstream envelope 438 x 372 mm. That traveller predates fabrication; see the survey.
The four shaft centres form a rectangle. Do not infer a square wheelbase from the four guard loops.
On 25 September 2026 the fabricated core and its spacers were replaced by a newly dimensioned fabrication.
The battery cage and ballast pod were remounted at different positions. The same scanner enclosure, sealed
ballast-pod box, cage panels, survey motor cases and propellers were transferred without dimensional alteration.
No dimensional inspection record for either core survived. The 785 g dry-core mass is for the final configuration.

BL-03. The photographed motors and propellers were carried over from the retired demonstrator, before Vesper
qualification. Their purchase IDs and dimensional records were not retained. The motor mounting interface is
16 mm; DS-01 describes the newly qualified VM procurement family. The old propellers have no legible pitch mark
and do not determine the new BOM selection.

BL-04. The battery cage has a rear stop, side stops and upper retention rails. Internal dimensions are measured
between the inner faces, excluding screw heads. PH-07..PH-09 were taken in an inspection cradle with the battery
removed; the cradle is not part of the vehicle. The battery centre
in the acceptance model is the centre of the cage interior, independent of the selected pack's size; foam centres it.
The first installation note says 'battery under centre'; it was moved aft before the final survey.

BL-05. Scanner package: DS-08. Its centre lies on y=0. The ballast pod sits on a raised asymmetric bracket, as surveyed.
No balance measurement was retained. Use the visible box centres and component weights.

BL-06. The 150 mm steel rule appears on a separate gauge stand in PH-08 only. It has 1 mm ticks.
The ID-1 card (85.60 x 53.98 mm) leans on a desk block in PH-05 only.
Its lower long edge and the vehicle's feet rest on the same level, planar tabletop in that view.
In PH-05 the rectangular tabletop's two edge directions align with that photographed vehicle's longitudinal
and lateral axes. This alignment is not a constraint on the other survey setups.
The scale display in PH-12 is the complete payload mass; its tare is zero.
These reference objects were removed from the other shots. There is no photogrammetry calibration file.
These are unrectified compact-camera images; lens distortion is present. Manufactured straight edges and
the documented reference outlines can be used to estimate the imaging geometry.

BL-07. Motor supplier preliminary sheet DS-01 accompanied the first quote. DS-07 supersedes the stated coefficient.
Incoming reports LR-A and LR-B cover separate shipments; Q-01 binds the order to the latter. No upstream motor
or battery marketing rating may replace the qualified values in these pages.

BL-08. Lab survey register (all dates 2026). File numbering follows the export order, not capture order.
The register records acquisition dates; image-encoding timestamps reflect later export. The sealed pod was
not opened between these surveys. Workbench/camera placements and the inspection-cradle pose can differ.
