# Component provenance and adaptation

AGiPix source: https://github.com/SasaKuruppuarachchi/agipix/tree/147a736b9fea7ac0e8d08c9a5fb0b90e5baa224a (CC0-1.0; licence retained in `kit/AGIPIX_LICENSE.txt`). The lower chassis, guard deck, compute cover and sensor cage retain the original manufacturing contours and stock thicknesses. Camera, Cube Orange, LiDAR, propeller and extracted component surfaces retain original dimensions. Source file hashes, coordinate transforms and collision-processing records are in `kit/kit.json` and `kit/provenance/`.

The repository's CAD instructions also link the author's public Agipix_v2 design at https://a360.co/4gY1h8M. Named closed STEP solids from that version recover surfaces missing from the merged STL export. Their geometry was compared against the pinned repository exports before use. These provide the original ESC, LiPo pack, motor base and flight-controller carrier/support geometry. A changed landing-gear design in the public CAD was not substituted for the pinned 66 mm source leg. The kit retains the original leg surfaces; its collision-only interface closure is documented separately.

Original facet or STEP face colors are retained where supplied. The battery's black wrap and silver label band are an appearance adaptation of the author's untextured pack, without text or brand; its shape is unchanged. Roughness and metallic parameters are rendering approximations. These are the author's CAD representations, not scanned hardware or independent certifications of exact retail variants. The computing component includes its source board, module, heatsink and connectors. The flight-controller unit bundles the source Cube, carrier and small captive mounting supports; the supports are identified separately from the commercial electronics.

xArm 7 and gripper: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/f3b6013629c7b94f8ee93fd977b161fa8350f9bc/ufactory_xarm7 (BSD-3-Clause; licence retained in `xarm7/LICENSE`). The original joint chain, gripper linkage and bounded actuators are used.

This is an adapted mechanical assembly kit. It uses a raised fixture, captive panel spacers and revised avionics/battery/sensor positions. Detailed components are not rescaled to fit earlier envelopes. Masses are declared nominal simulation values; geometric centers of mass and inertia use the documented source-solid or closed-volume assumptions, not measured material distributions. Screws, damping films and small captive supports are bundled with their parent components. The LiDAR's integrated cable, strain relief and gland boot and the battery's balance and power leads with their connectors (JST-XH, XT60) are removed, because the task covers mechanical assembly only; the LiDAR's gland collar is capped flat and the battery's lead exits are filled. Flexible-wire deformation, soldering, electrical operation and flight are outside the task.

Two sets of task-captive hardware adapt the sensor bay to this layout. The guard deck carries two TPU sensor-cage cradles (70 % infill, the source print settings), one under each sensor cheek: a rear block flush with the deck top under the cheek's straight bottom edge, a pocket for its bottom hook and a flange screwed under the deck. They replace the source's rubber-damped cage mount, which has no counterpart in the adapted upright cage. The camera unit bundles the two source `front_cam_bush` TPU bushes, on the board's bottom hole pair, with a task-captive 4 mm CF camera bridge: a 48 x 38 mm mount plate behind the board, which stands in for the source `cage_mid_f` front plate, and a crossbar that bridges the deck's sensor-bay front opening. Geometry, materials and masses are in `kit/kit.json` (`captive_hardware`) and `kit/provenance/sensor_bay_mounts.json`.

Visual meshes and collision pieces are separate. Closed-source convex decomposition is used where suitable, with documented collision-only repairs for source exports that lack interfaces. Propeller proxies preserve the original 13-sided shaft bore and actual motor shoulder. Motor and landing-strut mounts have four equivalent mounting orientations; each motor's bell and shaft remain on a separate unpowered hinge. Propellers attach to that rotor. CW and CCW propellers retain distinct geometry.

The two guard laminations form one supplied panel. Follow the supplied manual for this kit's arrangement and captive hardware; upstream vehicle illustrations document provenance rather than this workcell's placement.
